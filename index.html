Trader Takeaway

What should a trader watch next?

Prioritize developments that can change the physical balance: supply disruptions, OPEC+ decisions, inventory surprises, refinery outages, shipping constraints, weather and changes in demand expectations. The key question is whether today's move is a temporary headline shock or a change in the underlying supply-demand balance.
